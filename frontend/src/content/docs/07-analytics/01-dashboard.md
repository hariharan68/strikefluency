# Dashboard

The dashboard is deliberately arranged so that **discipline is the headline and P&L is secondary**. That ordering is the product's opinion: watching money moment to moment makes you worse, watching process makes you better.

It has three tabs: **Dashboard**, **Advanced Dashboard** and **Reports**.

## Discipline hero

The top of the page is a score ring with your current discipline score, coloured green above 80, amber above 50, red below.

Alongside it:

- A greeting and a **coaching line** that changes at the 90, 70 and 40 thresholds
- Your **streak** badge
- Your **tier** badge with current capital
- `N clean trades to next tier`
- A shortcut to the trading desk

## Today's guardrails

The most practically useful panel in the app. It tells you where you stand against your own limits *right now*:

| Guardrail | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Trades left today** | `X of max` with a progress bar |
| **Daily loss cap** | The rupee cap, percentage used, amber above 60% |
| **Revenge cooldown** | Time remaining, or `Clear`, or a note that a lock arms after an SL hit |

Below these, on/off pills for the four boolean rules — mandatory stop-loss, no averaging down, no direction flip, mandatory setup tag — each with hover text explaining why it exists.

Checking this panel before you place a trade takes three seconds and prevents most rejections.

## Calm stats

Four numbers, deliberately understated:

- **Virtual Balance** with a sparkline
- **Today's P&L**
- **Open (Unrealized)**
- **All-time P&L**

They are called calm stats because they are styled to not shout. The visual hierarchy is intentional — the score ring dominates, the money does not.

## Charts

- **Daily P&L** bars, labelled *"Practice ledger"*
- **Equity curve** area chart, labelled *"Cumulative"*

## Today's discipline log

The day's violations, reframed as coaching rather than scolding. Shows `Clean run` when there are none, or `N slips` with the details.

## Practice summary

Streak, score, win days and loss days, plus a nudge to journal today's trades.

**Win days and loss days** are a better metric than win rate early on. A trader with a 45% win rate but more green days than red is managing risk well.

## The Reports tab

The third tab shows your discipline report — score ring, compliance metrics, an editable rules table and recent violations.

Note this is **not** the same as the `/reports` page in the profile menu, which is an empty placeholder. The naming collision is confusing and worth knowing about.

## How to use it

Open the dashboard before you trade, not after. Check the guardrails panel, note how many trades you have left and how much of your loss cap is used, and let that inform how selective you need to be.

Checking it afterward to see the damage is the less useful direction.
